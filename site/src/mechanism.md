# Why oxides

Abstention value and UIP–label disagreement localize in oxide chemistry, consistent with oxide reference energetics (including missing Hubbard-<em>U</em> treatment) and fitting difficulty. The reading is not reducible to MP2020 corrections or a coordination-environment proxy.

## Four probes, kept separate

**Valence.** High formal valence carries the oxide-localized benefit. Mid valence does not.

**Cation class.** Within oxides the benefit does not localize to a single cation family as a deployable rule.

**MP2020 sensitivity.** Correction-sensitive cation classes do not own the within-oxide signal.

**Post-hoc status.** These panels are descriptive probes after the Stage-1/2 claim. They do not validate DFT labels.
