# Potentials

Selective abstention at a matched surfaced-candidate budget is a chemistry-localized diagnostic of UIP–label disagreement. CHGNet shows a +7.7 precision-point advantage for oxides over halides. Low-base-rate normalisation amplifies the same contrast to +0.90 DAF (95% CI [0.74, 1.04]); the precision-point interaction is primary.
